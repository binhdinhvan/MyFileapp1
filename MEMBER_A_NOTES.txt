=========================================================
 THANH VIEN A - DUYET & DIEU HUONG FILE (ban bo sung)
=========================================================

Nguyen tac: CHI THEM code, KHONG xoa dong nao cua ban goc.
Tat ca tinh nang moi gan vao man hinh chinh (MainActivity).

------------------------------------------------
1) TINH NANG DA THEM
------------------------------------------------
- Breadcrumb: thanh duong dan bam duoc (Root > A > B ...), tu cuon sang
  phai theo thu muc hien tai. Bam vao 1 chang de nhay thang toi do.
- Sap xep (Sort): Ten A-Z / Z-A, Kich thuoc, Ngay sua (tang/giam).
  Thu muc luon dung truoc file.
- Tim kiem (Search): loc theo ten trong THU MUC HIEN TAI (khong phan biet
  hoa thuong). Nut X de xoa nhanh. (Xem muc "Ghi chu" ve tim de quy.)
- Doi List / Grid: nut "Grid"/"List" o goc phai; Grid hien 3 cot.
- Drawer chon storage: nut ☰ o header mo ngan keo trai, liet ke
  "Bo nho trong" + "The SD / USB" (kem dung luong trong/tong).

------------------------------------------------

3) FILE CU BI CHINH (chi THEM dong)
------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------
4) DEPENDENCY / MANIFEST
------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------
